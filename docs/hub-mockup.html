How this would work

Only the gold-labelled bits are writing. The blue sections are assembled at build time from notes you'd be making anyway, so logging a session, posting a job or updating a character is just writing a note with a few lines of frontmatter and publishing it.

---
type: job
status: open
patron: Hedda Stane
reward: 50 gp
hex: 59
---
# Burnt farmsteads north of Carlinholt
Hedda wants to know...
---
type: session
date: 2026-09-10
party: [Ysolde, Brak, Tam]
hexes: [79, 89, 101]
summary: drove off a goblin scouting
  party, found the south bridge cut
---
# The bridges below the Wrym
---
type: character
player: (player name)
class: Ranger
level: 3
status: alive
---
